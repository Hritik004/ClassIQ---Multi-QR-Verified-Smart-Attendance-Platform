From the build log

Why the QR code alone wasn't enough

Static QR codes get screenshotted and shared in group chats within minutes — so ClassIQ was built as a Flask-based smart attendance system around QR codes that regenerate on every session, making a saved screenshot or proxy scan useless.

Code alone doesn't stop proxy attendance, so verification happens in layers: OTP confirmation ties the scan to the person, and IP whitelisting ties it to the room. On top of that sit role-based dashboards for students and faculty, with attendance monitoring that updates as scans come in.
